# Nobel Prize Nomination Archive Explorer

A Streamlit app to explore the Nobel Prize Nomination Archive, covering nominations from 1901 through 1974.

**Live app:** [nobelnominations.streamlit.app](https://nobelnominations.streamlit.app/)

## Features

- **Search by Name** — Look up any nominee or nominator in the archive
- **Browse by Category/Year** — Explore nominations across Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, and Peace
- **Statistics** — Distribution of nominations for laureates (log-normal fit), most nominated non-winners, and cross-category comparisons

## Data

All data is sourced from the [Nobel Prize Nomination Archive](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/archive/). The archive contains nomination records made public after a 50-year secrecy period, currently covering 1901--1974.
